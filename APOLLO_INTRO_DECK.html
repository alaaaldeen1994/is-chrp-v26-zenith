The Problem

Longevity Research

Developing a longevity therapy takes years of failed cycles. Each experiment takes weeks — and most fail. There is no software today that predicts what will work before you try it.

  • A single drug program takes 5–10+ years to reach clinical trials
  • Each failed experiment cycle wastes weeks or months
  • Success rates are under 2% per attempt
  • Scientists can't track all the genes involved — thousands interact at once
  • No tool today predicts if a therapy is safe before testing it
