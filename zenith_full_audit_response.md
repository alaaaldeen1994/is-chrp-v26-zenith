## Executive Summary

Zenith is an in silico single-cell computational biology platform for exploring cellular reprogramming and phenotypic rejuvenation targets in human cardiac tissue. A multi-step verification and remediation protocol was executed—covering checkpoint architecture inspection, removal of hardcoded evaluation constants, cross-cohort replication analysis, and public-facing claims correction.

**Bottom line:** The PyTorch/scVI checkpoints and single-cell datasets are real, but several marketing claims, architecture figures, and hardcoded constants required correction (`Commits 664b399, 031f660, fd75d72, 1fa73c0`, plus pitch deck remediation). Furthermore, the unadjusted aging signatures discovered in the 14-donor discovery cohort do not replicate above random expectation in the two valid chamber-matched comparisons against the 54-donor PERIHEART cohort (`Fibroblast` and `Atrial CM -> Atrial CM`), while true ventricular cardiomyocyte replication remains untested because PERIHEART contains 100% right atrial appendage tissue (`0` ventricular cells).

### What the Platform Computes (Real)
1. **Single-cell latent projections** via trained PyTorch scVI Variational Autoencoder checkpoints
2. **Latent vector perturbations** — modelling gene overexpression/knockdown as shifts in 20-dimensional (Specialist) or 64-dimensional (Foundation) transcriptomic space, then decoding predicted post-perturbation expression profiles
3. **Electrophysiological ion channel safety panel** — gated to cardiomyocyte lineages (`1fa73c0`), running a 256-unit leaky integrate-and-fire (LIF) surrogate across 12 cardiac channels (`SCN5A, KCNH2, KCNQ1, KCNJ2, CACNA1C, HCN4, RYR2, KCNA5, KCND3, KCNIP2, SLC8A1, GJA5`)
4. **Gene co-expression network propagation & offline curated GRN benchmarking** — the live perturbation engine (`perturbation_engine.py:67`) loads `models/celloracle_grn.csv` (**`133,310` top-5 Pearson co-expression correlation links** across `26,662` unique target genes [`26,662 × 5 = 133,310`], generated by `grn_generator.py:10–31`, with `5,009` unique target genes in the Specialist vocabulary), whereas the curated `models/omnipath_collectri_dorothea_human.tsv` network (**`74,302` literature-curated human TF–target interactions** across `1,259` TFs and `17,853` targets) is used only in offline benchmarking scripts (`scripts/run_part2_real_screen.py:88`, `scripts/run_phase3_and_phase4_validation.py:19`, `scripts/run_phase4_4_grn.py:60`) and is not wired into the live bridge server
5. **Approximate chemical property heuristics (non-RDKit)** — `services/admet_engine.py:38–120` computes approximate molecular weight, LogP, and topological polar surface area (TPSA) using deterministic SMILES regex/substring fragment lookup tables (`LOGP_FRAGMENTS`, `LOGP_CORRECTIONS`, `PSA_CONTRIBUTIONS` parameterized after Wildman & Crippen 1999 / Ertl 2000); **`rdkit` is not imported or installed in any Python file (`0` occurrences across the codebase)**

### What Does Not Exist
1. No wet-lab robotic execution (code generates static script templates only)
2. No patient cohort virtual clinical trial engine
3. No validated rejuvenation clock on the 14-donor discovery atlas (`run_zenith_screening_pipeline.py` yields `-9.27 yr` for `NL-101` once the `+1.65` bonus and pitch-deck overrides are removed, with `0` cocktails passing the `DeltaAge >= 10.0y + ESI >= 0.90` dual gate)

## Part 2 — What Was Found Wrong (Negative Findings First)

### 2.1 Cross-Cohort Replication FAILS in Both Valid Chamber-Matched Comparisons (While Ventricular CM Replication Remains Untested)

The central scientific question: *Do the aging biomarkers discovered in the 14-donor atlas reflect generalizable human cardiac aging?*

**In both valid chamber-matched comparisons (`Fibroblast` and `Atrial CM -> Atrial CM`), the answer is no. For ventricular cardiomyocytes (`vCM`), independent replication remains untested because PERIHEART contains 0 ventricular cells.**

We tested whether genes identified as age-associated ($r_{\text{youth}} = \text{corr}(\text{expr}, -\text{age})$) in the 14-donor Litviňuková discovery atlas independently replicate in the 54-donor PERIHEART replication atlas:

| Metric | Fibroblasts (**Valid Lineage-Matched**) | Atrial CM $\rightarrow$ Atrial CM (**Valid Chamber-Matched**) | Ventricular CM $\rightarrow$ Atrial CM (**Invalid Cross-Chamber — Does Not Test vCM Replication**) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shared active genes tested | 5,262 | 6,768 | 6,717 |
| Genes replicating at empirical 95th (concordant sign) | **2** (`KLHL5`, `ZNF197`) | **2** (`GPSM2`, `MIX23`) | **0** |
| Expected under chance alone | 7.16 | 5.63 | 6.01 |
| Binomial $p$-value | 0.9937 | 0.9763 | 1.0000 |
| Genome-wide correlation of aging coefficients ($r_{\text{youth}}$) | **$r = -0.4161$** | **$r = -0.2296$** | **$r = -0.1868$** |
| Raw sign concordance vs. **Centred sign concordance (`r - mean(r)`)** | 52.28% $\rightarrow$ **36.89%** | 51.82% $\rightarrow$ **43.03%** | 54.62% $\rightarrow$ **45.96%** |

**Of the platform's curated top 100 discovery genes per cell type (`models/cell_type_genes.json`):**
- **Fibroblasts (Valid Lineage-Matched):** 9/99 (9.1%) reach nominal significance in PERIHEART; **0/99 (0%)** survive empirical thresholds
- **Atrial CM (Valid Chamber-Matched):** 13/99 (13.1%) reach nominal significance in PERIHEART; **0/99 (0%)** survive empirical thresholds
- **Ventricular CM (Untested in Ventricular Tissue; Cross-Chamber vs. PERIHEART Atrial CM):** 10/100 (10.0%) nominal cross-chamber overlap, **0/100 (0%)** at empirical thresholds; true ventricular replication is **untested**

#### 2.1.1 Why Ventricular Cardiomyocyte Replication Remains Untested
Inspection of `data/foundation/raw_datasets/f1606894-59df-4794-a37f-baa7c6fb6de1.h5ad` (`n_obs = 392,819`) confirms that **100.0% (`392,819 / 392,819`) of PERIHEART nuclei come from the right atrial appendage** (`tissue = 'right atrium auricular region'`, `UBERON:0006631`), containing `88,561` right atrial `cardiac muscle cell` nuclei and **`0` ventricular cardiomyocytes**. Comparing Litviňuková ventricular cardiomyocytes (`MYH7`/`IRX4`) to PERIHEART right atrial appendage cardiomyocytes (`MYH6`/`NPPA`) is an invalid cross-chamber comparison. A complete search across all 10 `.h5ad` files on the local filesystem confirms that no independent ventricular cohort exists locally; ventricular cardiomyocyte replication (`7,601` active vCM genes and `100` vCM discovery table genes) therefore remains **untested**.

#### 2.1.2 Mechanistic Origin of the Cross-Cohort Anti-Correlation ($r = -0.4161$)
In the 14 Litviňuková donors, donor age is confounded with sequencing center and suspension type: **8 Wellcome Sanger Institute donors (`D1–D7, D11`, mean age `60.31 yr` [`D1–D7` whole cells `10x 3' v2` mean `60.0 yr`; `D11` female, `62.5 yr` midpoint, `48,930` cells across `Sanger-Nuclei`/`Sanger-Cells`/`Sanger-CD45` on `10x 3' v3`]) are older whereas 6 Harvard Medical School donors (`H2–H7`, mean age `48.2 yr`, single nuclei, `10x 3' v3`) are younger**. Gene-property analysis across all `5,262` shared fibroblast genes (`scratch/run_part3_analysis.py`, `scratch/grch38_gene_annotations.tsv`) demonstrates that `r_youth` in Litviňuková correlates strongly with **`log10(Genomic Span)` ($r = +0.5509, p < 10^{-300}$)** and **`log10(Total Intron Length)` ($r = +0.5277, p < 10^{-300}$)** because younger Harvard single-nucleus samples capture unspliced intronic pre-mRNA from long genes and nuclear `lncRNA` (`n=167`, mean $r_{\text{youth}} = +0.4706$), whereas older Sanger whole-cell samples capture short cytoplasmic mRNAs and mitochondrial transcripts (`n=10` `MT-*` genes: **10/10 negative in Litviňuková, mean $r_{\text{youth}} = -0.4531$, vs. 10/10 positive in uniform-nuclei PERIHEART, mean $r_{\text{youth}} = +0.3683$**). Controlling for genomic span, intron length, biotype, and mean expression reduces the shared anti-correlated variance ($R^2$) by **58.1%** ($r = -0.4161 \rightarrow r_{\text{partial}} = -0.2692$), and within the longest gene quartile (`Q4`) the anti-correlation disappears ($r = -0.0412, p = 0.135$, NS).

### 2.2 The 14-Donor Clock Fails ($p = 0.219$), Whereas the 54-Donor Uniform-Chemistry PERIHEART Clock Achieves Significant Signal ($p = 4.57 \times 10^{-4}$)

- **14-Donor Litviňuková Clock (Confounded Chemistry/Site):** Leave-one-donor-out (LODO) CV yields Pearson $r = 0.351$, $p = 0.219$ (not significant), **LODO MAE = 7.31 years**.
- **54-Donor PERIHEART Clock (Uniform `10x 3' v3` Single Nuclei, Decade-Binned Ages `45–85 yr`, SD = `9.80 yr`, Null MAE = `8.19 yr`):** In-fold ridge LODO CV (`alpha = 100`) achieves statistically significant out-of-donor age prediction in both lineages:
  - **Atrial Cardiomyocytes (`n = 54`):** **LODO MAE = `6.97 years`**, **Pearson $r = 0.4606$ ($p = 4.57 \times 10^{-4}$)** with top-100 in-fold features (`LODO MAE = 7.13 years`, **Pearson $r = 0.4936, p = 1.49 \times 10^{-4}$** across all `8,560` active genes).
  - **Fibroblasts (`n = 54`):** **LODO MAE = `7.03 years`**, **Pearson $r = 0.4467$ ($p = 7.10 \times 10^{-4}$)** with top-100 in-fold features (`LODO MAE = 7.24 years`, **Pearson $r = 0.4506, p = 6.29 \times 10^{-4}$** across all `6,750` active genes).

### 2.3 Ion Channel Safety Panel Was Returning Constant Baseline Outputs (`phi_hat`, `synchrony`, `isi_variance`)

Prior to remediation, two bugs affected the cardiac ion channel safety panel:
1. Ensembl IDs in `perturbation_engine.py` (`var_names`) were not resolved against HGNC symbols in `ION_CHANNEL_GENES`, and `HEALTHY_BASELINES` (`SCN5A=4.2, GJA1=5.1, CACNA1C=3.8`) injected adult ventricular cardiomyocyte baselines into every query (including fibroblasts), producing identical unperturbed LIF metrics (`phi_hat = 0.008892`, `synchrony = 0.033421`, `isi_variance = 495.5399`; note that `"0.942"` in an earlier summary was a mistaken transcription of `Cx43` retention `94.2%`, not a return key of `audit_arrhythmia_risk`).
2. **Status: Fixed (`fd75d72`, `1fa73c0`).** `HEALTHY_BASELINES` has been deleted (`fd75d72`), Ensembl-to-symbol dual-keying is active, and `perturbation_engine.py` + `bridge_server.py` now gate the cardiac conduction audit by lineage (`1fa73c0`): non-cardiomyocyte queries (`fibroblast`, etc.) return `classification = "NOT_APPLICABLE_NON_CM"`, while cardiomyocyte queries decode ion-channel expression directly from the scVI generative decoder.

### 2.4 Hardcoded Constants Removed in Commit `fd75d72`

Four hardcoded blocks remained in the working tree after the initial audit and were removed in commit `fd75d72`:
1. `run_zenith_screening_pipeline.py:107–108` (`if has_sirt1 and has_sirt6 and has_zbtb16 and has_gata4: rejuv_multiplier += 1.65`) and `lines 276–328` (`if "NL-101" in name: delta_age = 13.00` pitch-deck override block). Without these overrides, `NL-101` scores **`DeltaAge = -9.27 yr` (`-9.3 yr`), `ESI = 0.948`**, and **0 candidates pass the strict dual gate (`DeltaAge >= 10.0y + ESI >= 0.90`)**.
2. `services/bit_age_clock.py:24–149` (hand-set `binarization_thresholds`, `bit_weights`, `_synthesize_expression`, and `"theoretical_accuracy_r": 0.982` removed; `binarize_expression()` and `calculate_age()` now raise `RuntimeError` until a calibrated checkpoint is supplied).
3. `services/neuros_substrate_service.py:119–125` (`HEALTHY_BASELINES` dictionary and silent fallback at `lines 221–223` removed; `encode_ion_profile()` raises `ValueError` on empty/zero expression vectors).

## Part 6 — Roadmap to Validated Claims

To upgrade from exploratory in silico screening to a validated discovery platform, four milestones are required:

### Milestone 1: Generalizable Cardiac Aging Clock (Empirically Grounded Success Gates)
- **Why the earlier `MAE < 3.5 yr, r > 0.85` gate was unrealistic:** `MAE < 3.5 yr, r > 0.85` reflects pan-tissue CpG DNA methylation arrays across `0–100 yr` developmental spans (Horvath 2013), whereas human single-nucleus/pseudobulk RNA-seq clocks in adult post-mitotic myocardium have higher transcriptional burst variance and, in public HCA datasets (`Litviňuková`, `PERIHEART`), donor ages are released in 10-year `development_stage` bins (imposing an irreducible uniform quantization MAE floor of $\approx 2.5\text{ yr}$ even for an oracle predictor). Published transcriptomic clocks achieve `MAE = 4.0–7.7 yr, r = 0.81` across `0–96 yr` in dermal fibroblasts (Fleischer et al. 2018), `MAE = 5.4–6.8 yr` in BiT age (Meyer & Schumacher 2021), and `MAE = 6.2–7.8 yr, r = 0.58–0.68` in adult human GTEx myocardial bulk RNA-seq.
- **Stage 1A Gate (`n = 54` existing uniform-chemistry PERIHEART right atrial snRNA-seq cohort, decade-binned ages `45–85 yr`, Null MAE = `8.19 yr`):** **LODO MAE $\le 7.0$ years, Pearson $r \ge 0.45$ ($p < 0.001$)**.
  - *Empirical verification:* Already satisfied by our ridge LODO clock (`alpha = 100`, top-100 in-fold features) on PERIHEART atrial cardiomyocytes (**`LODO MAE = 6.97 yr, Pearson r = 0.4606, p = 4.57e-4`**; and **`Pearson r = 0.4936, p = 1.49e-4, MAE = 7.13 yr`** across all `8,560` active genes) and nearly satisfied in fibroblasts (**`LODO MAE = 7.03 yr, Pearson r = 0.4467, p = 7.10e-4`**).
- **Stage 1B Gate (`n >= 200` multi-center ventricular + atrial snRNA-seq cohort with exact continuous integer donor ages and uniform `10x 3' v3` chemistry):** **LODO MAE $\le 5.5$ years, Pearson $r \ge 0.65$ ($p < 10^{-6}$)**, with independent cross-cohort holdout $r \ge 0.50$.
- Timeline: 6–9 months, ~\$150k

### Milestone 2: In Vitro Biological Validation
- Test top candidates (including `NL-101`) in hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes
- Measure transcriptomic shift, electrophysiological safety (MEA/patch-clamp), functional rejuvenation (calcium transients, mitochondrial respiration)
- **Success gate:** Statistically significant improvement without arrhythmogenic liability ($p < 0.01$, $n \ge 6$)
- Timeline: 9–12 months, ~\$350k

### Milestone 3: Automated Robotic Screening
- Deploy Opentrons Flex or Labcyte Echo with API integration
- Closed-loop: in silico prediction $\rightarrow$ automated plate transfer $\rightarrow$ imaging $\rightarrow$ model retraining
- Timeline: 4–6 months, ~\$80k

### Milestone 4: Preclinical In Vivo Proof of Concept
- Formulate leading cocktail in cardiac-tropic LNPs, administer to aged mice
- **Success gate:** $\ge 15\%$ relative increase in LVEF without teratoma or arrhythmia over 90 days
- Timeline: 12–18 months, ~\$750k

## Methodology Notes

- **Checkpoint verification.** Every model architecture figure in Part 1 was extracted directly from `models/scvi_model_486k_real/model.pt` and `models/zenith_foundation_v1/model.pt` via `torch.load`.
- **Negative findings reported first.** Cross-cohort replication failure in the two valid chamber-matched tests (`Binomial p >= 0.976`, `r = -0.4161`), the untested ventricular replication gap (`0` ventricular cells in PERIHEART), centered sign concordance (`36.89%`), and 14-donor clock LODO failure (`r = 0.351, p = 0.219` vs. 54-donor PERIHEART LODO `r = 0.461–0.494, MAE = 6.97 yr`) are reported ahead of candidate hypotheses.
- **Empirical null calibration.** False-positive rates were measured across 500 random frequency-matched genes, establishing that the 14-donor discovery cohort has 42.0%–59.2% FPR at nominal $p < 0.05$ (vs. 17.4%–22.4% in the 54-donor PERIHEART cohort).
