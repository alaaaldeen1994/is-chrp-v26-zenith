Abstract

Cellular aging in the human myocardium leads to irreversible functional decline and heart failure. Conventional partial reprogramming using Yamanaka factors (OSKM) presents significant clinical barriers, including oncogenic risk and loss of somatic identity. We present a novel cardiac-specific rejuvenation protocol discovered via scVI deep-learning analysis of the Human Cell Atlas.

1. Introduction

Current regenerative medicine focuses on pluripotency, yet the heart requires in-situ rejuvenation. Our objective was to identify a minimalist transcription factor cocktail capable of resetting the epigenetic clock (Horvath-aligned) while maintaining cardiomyocyte lineage stability.

Goal: Minimize genetic load while maximizing Epigenetic Stability Index (ESI).
