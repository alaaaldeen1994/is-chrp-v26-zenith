Questions She'll Ask
— Part 1

Read these out loud. Know the answers.

❓ "What have you actually built?"

We have a live AI system — Zenith v26.1 with 285M parameters. It's fully tested. We also have an automated lab connection so the AI can drive physical experiments. We're past the prototype stage.

❓ "Have you tested this in real cells?"

Our current results are computer-based — all validated against 150,000 real cell samples. Physical lab validation is exactly what Series A funding unlocks. We're clear about where we are.

❓ "Who are your competitors?"

Altos Labs and Retro Bio are wet labs — they could actually become our customers. In the software layer, no one has our level of resolution or safety prediction. We're first.

⚠️ Important

On the "real cells" question — be honest.

Don't say you've done physical experiments if you haven't. VCs check everything. Say: "Computer-validated, physical testing is the next funded step."

This is the strong answer.

Stay calm. Don't get defensive.

Stay Sharp

Do & Don't

Simple rules for a great call.

✅ DO

  • Be honest — especially about what's tested and what isn't
  • Slow down — pause after each section, let her speak
  • Listen — write down what she says
  • Name Apollo — show you know who they are
  • End with a clear ask — meeting #2, specific and direct
  • Say "that's fair" if she challenges you

❌ DON'T

  • Claim physical results you don't have
  • Use too much jargon without explaining it first
  • Talk about the 7B model as if it's built — it's a roadmap goal
  • Rush — slow is confident, fast is nervous
  • Get defensive when she pushes back
  • Leave without agreeing on a next step
🏆

She's watching you as much as the idea. Be honest, calm, and direct. That earns a second meeting.
