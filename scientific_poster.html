1. Abstract & The Translational Gap

Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of global mortality. While partial reprogramming holds immense therapeutic promise, conventional Yamanaka factors (Oct4, Sox2, Klf4, c-Myc / OSKM) trigger severe cardiotoxicity: causing loss of cardiomyocyte identity, fatal arrhythmias, and teratoma formation. Furthermore, 90% of cardiac therapeutics fail in clinical trials due to rodent electrophysiological divergence (mouse hearts beat at 600 bpm vs human 60 bpm).

The Bottleneck: Traditional animal models fail to predict human cardiac electrophysiology. A human single-cell in-silico foundation model with hard biophysical safety gates is required.
