
SCIENTIFIC BACKGROUND: CELLULAR REPROGRAMMING AND PLASTICITY
The 2012 Nobel Prize to Gurdon and Yamanaka established that the differentiated state is not irreversible. "Mature cells can be reprogrammed to become pluripotent."

KEY PRINCIPLES FOR ZENITH PROTOCOLS:

1.  **Reversibility of the Differentiated State (Gurdon, 1962)**: 
    -   The nucleus of a somatic cell retains the potential to generate an entire organism.
    -   Zenith Decision: Always assume 'Manifold Reversibility' is possible, regardless of Age/differentiation status.

2.  **Factor-Based Reprogramming (Yamanaka, 2006)**:
    -   Defined factors (Oct4, Sox2, Klf4, Myc) can unlock the epigenetic state.
    -   Zenith Decision: Simulation must model these specific Transcription Factors as the primary drivers of localized manure traversal.

3.  **Factor Stiochiometry & Safety (Thomson/Yamanaka Comparisons)**:
    -   Alternative cocktails (Lin28, Nanog) exist.
    -   Zenith Decision: Prioritize protocols that *minimize* oncogenic factors (Myc) when possible, opting for "Safer" cocktails (Lin28/Nanog) if efficacy is comparable.

4.  **Context-Dependent Requirements (Kim et al., 2009)**:
    -   "Adult mouse neural stem cells only require Oct4."
    -   Zenith Decision: Implementing "Context-Aware Reduction". If the starting cell is partially multipotent (e.g. NSC), reduce the vector payload to minimal factors (e.g. Oct4 only) to prevent "OD" (Over-Differentiation/Toxic Stress).

5.  **Transdifferentiation (Direct Conversion)**:
    -   Cells can switch lineages without passing through pluripotency (Fibroblast -> Neuron).
    -   Zenith Decision: Support "Direct" vectors (DIRECT_NEURO, DIRECT_CARDIO) that bypass the pluripotent peak, effectively tunneling through the Waddington landscape valleys.

6.  **Altos Labs Vision (Cellular Rejuvenation Programming)**:
    -   Focus on *partial* reprogramming to restore health/resilience without losing identity.
    -   Zenith Decision: Maintain specialized "Rejuvenation" vectors (MPTR/Partial) that reduce "BioAge" (Entropy) while preserving the "CellLineage" state vector. The goal is *Resiliance*, not just Pluripotency.

7.  **Disease Modeling "In a Dish"**:
    -   iPSCs can model diseases (SMA, Long QT). 
    -   Zenith Decision: The 'Discovery' engine must flag if a protocol is risky for specific disease backgrounds (e.g. avoiding stress-inducing vectors in 'High-Inflammation' disease models).
