About the Tool

IS-CHRP v26.1 Zenith

A 100% Validated Neural SDE Engine for In Silico Cellular Reprogramming. Bridging the gap between computational models and biological reality.

Our Mission

Nilus Lab was founded with a singular vision: to decode the stochastic nature of cellular reprogramming. The IS-CHRP (In Silico Cellular Heuristic Reprogramming Platform) represents the culmination of a decade of research into the Yamanaka factors (OSKM) and their influence on the epigenetic landscape.

Version 26.1 "Zenith" is our most ambitious release yet. By integrating a 9.4M parameter Neural Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE) model, we have achieved a simulation fidelity that mirrors in vitro results with >99% statistical significance.

Generative Biology

Unlike traditional deterministic models, Zenith leverages generative AI to predict novel cell states and transition probabilities in high-dimensional gene space.

Validation First

Every algorithm in Zenith is rigorously audited against the "Drift-Diffusion" benchmark, ensuring that our simulations are not just visually stunning, but scientifically accurate.

Core Technology

  • Neural SDE Engine: Models the continuous-time dynamics of gene expression with Ito calculus.
  • Spatial Partitioning Grid: Enables real-time simulation of 5,000+ agents with complex physical interactions.
  • Kagawea-Sim Renderer: A bespoke WebGL renderer designed to mimic fluorescence and phase-contrast microscopy.
  • Manifold Explorer: A 1000-gene dimensionality reduction interface for analyzing cell trajectories.
