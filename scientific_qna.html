Methodology & Verification Q&A

Explore the peer-reviewed mathematical, biological, and deep learning foundations behind the Zenith Generative Reprogramming Engine.

The biological ground truth for the healthy cardiac reference baseline is derived from the landmark publication "Cells of the adult human heart" (Litviňuková et al. 2020, Nature). This study sequenced exactly 486,134 single cells and nuclei across 14 biologically independent healthy donors.

To ensure stable local computations, the system maps vectors against a highly curated 18,641-cell representative subset that retains the full 26,662-gene transcriptome. This allows your local scripts to retain low-abundance, biologically critical transcription factors and structural markers (such as TNNT2 and MYH7) that are lost in pruned models.

The Human Cell Atlas (HCA) is the global scientific consortium of research laboratories that generated the physical biological tissues and sequenced the transcriptomes.

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) CELLxGENE Discover Portal is the computational host and data distribution pipeline. It standardizes, indexes, and serves the HCA heart study (specifically under dataset UUID a7f6822d-0e0e-451e-9858-af81392fcb9b). The Zenith platform retrieves HCA biological references programmatically using the official CELLxGENE API.

The active production model (v28.0 GOLD) was trained on the integrated 500,000-cell dataset (integrating the 486K cell HCA adult heart atlas with cells from the Tabula Sapiens - Heart and PERIHEART datasets).

Due to the computational density of calculating multi-study batch integrations, training was performed using Google Colab GPU-accelerated cloud systems (NVIDIA T4 GPUs). By executing minibatch gradient descent with an optimized batch size of 512, the model compiled the deep 30-dimensional latent manifold ($z=30$, $N_{\text{layers}}=2$, $N_{\text{hidden}}=256$) over 400 epochs in under 25 minutes.

The resulting neural network weights (53.7 MB) are loaded actively by the backend to handle real-time user-interface queries.

Zenith leverages deep generative models using the scVI (single-cell Variational Inference) framework (Lopez et al. 2018). It models discrete gene expression counts ($x_{ig}$) using a Negative Binomial (NB) likelihood distribution: $$\log p(x_{ig} | z_i, s_i) = \text{NB}(x_{ig}; \mu_{ig}(z_i, s_i), \theta_g)$$ where batch effects ($s_i$) are mathematically factored out, leaving a pure biological latent representation ($z_i$).

Lineage transitions are modeled using **latent space vector arithmetic** (scGen, Lotfollahi et al. 2019). By computing the vector difference between the Fibroblast and Cardiomyocyte centroids, the system applies optimal-transport trajectories to simulate out-of-sample cellular perturbations under different factor doses.

The Zenith platform exposes only high-level statistical summaries, latent displacement indices, and gene enrichment scores via public web APIs. All proprietary deep learning weights, PyTorch layer structures, and raw Gene Regulatory Networks (GRNs) containing 133,310 links are securely executed inside our isolated backend containers.

This "Digital-to-Biological" (D2B) architecture allows researchers to query predictions and export automated Opentrons Flex robotic protocols without risking the exposure of Nilus Lab's proprietary intellectual property (IP).
