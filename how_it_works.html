02. Mastering the Left Sidebar

The left sidebar is your Intervention Engine. It allows you to manually override the AI and stress-test your rejuvenation cocktail.

1. Reprogramming Factors

Use the dropdown to select specific cocktails. If the AI discovery (Right Sidebar) is complete, your candidate will appear here as "Predicted Strategy". Click "Transfect Population" to apply the factors instantly.

2. Pathological Perturbations

Click "Induce Tumor" or "Induce SMA" to challenge your rejuvenated cells.
Goal: Ensure that cells rejuvenated in "Partial Mode" do NOT transition into a tumor state even under oncogenic stress.

3. Genomic Knockout (Stress Test)

Use this to delete safety genes like TP53. Click "Apply Knockout" to see if your 10-year HMVEC protocol remains non-oncogenic without tumor suppressors active.
