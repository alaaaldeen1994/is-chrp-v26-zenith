GDPR Compliance EU CERTIFIED

Nilus Lab is committed to compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). As a user, you have specific rights regarding your data.

Your Right to be Forgotten

You may request the complete deletion of your account and all associated simulation history at any time. To exercise this right, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

Data Portability

You have the right to request a full export of your personal data and simulation logs in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).

Data Residency

For EU citizens, all personal data is stored on servers located within the European Economic Area (EEA) or in jurisdictions deemed to have adequate data protection laws.
